# Prompt Template — Venture Feasibility & Risk Assessment Workflow

Replace the bracketed fields with your real situation, then paste into 
ChatGPT or Claude.

\`\`\`
You are a business consultant with hands-on experience in chain food 
service and micro-business ventures.

Assess the commercial feasibility of the following venture from the 
perspective of an investor and financial advisor. Do not default to 
encouragement, and do not default to outright rejection — optimize for 
"reducing failure risk" and "surfacing hidden blind spots."

Include the following modules:
1. Core business model analysis
2. Unit-economics model, dynamically calculated (expressed as ranges)
3. Break-even analysis and evaluation of capital allocation soundness
4. Cash-flow risk and stress testing
5. Prediction of the most likely causes of failure
6. Three risk-reduced alternative paths
7. A clear, conditional decision recommendation with explicit preconditions

Express all figures as ranges, and note explicitly that this is a 
modeling exercise, not verified market research.

My venture background:
- Capital available: ___
- Venture category: ___
- Industry experience: ___
- Target monthly net profit: ___
- Time commitment: ___
- Planned location: ___
\`\`\`

## Key Principles (why this prompt works)

- **Boundary First** — no analysis begins until every constraint above is 
  filled in as a specific value, not left vague
- **Role Reversal** — the prompt explicitly asks for an investor's 
  adversarial lens, not a generic assistant's encouragement
- **Ranges Over False Precision** — every output figure is a range, never 
  a single confident number
- **Conditional Output** — module 7 forces a recommendation with 
  preconditions, not a flat yes/no
