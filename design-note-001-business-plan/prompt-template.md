# Prompt Template — AI-Assisted Business Planning Workflow

Paste this directly into your AI tool of choice.

\`\`\`
# Role
You are a business transformation consultant with 10 years of experience,
specializing in D2C brand building and scaling independent service businesses.
Tone: objective, precise, high commercial logic. No filler. Go straight to the point.

# Context
The client currently relies on a third-party platform for client acquisition 
and delivery, with steady revenue but facing platform fees of 30–50% and no 
ownership of the client list. Goal: transition to an independent, direct-to-
client business with automated booking, payments, and their own website.

# Task
Write a complete, investor-grade Business Plan, structured in five modules,
formatted in Markdown with bold headers.

# Required structure and precision requirements

## I. Executive Summary
- Three core pain points: platform fees / no ownership of client list /
  no pricing power
- Transformation strategy: shift from a "labor model" to a "brand asset model"
- Three use-of-funds categories: digital infrastructure / content assets /
  operating reserve

## II. Solution & Product Matrix
A three-tier product structure:
1. High-ticket 1:1 service — primary cash flow, protects margin
2. Themed group offering — breaks the time ceiling, expands capacity
3. Asynchronous/productized content — near-zero marginal cost, passive income

If AI-assisted delivery is part of the offer, state both benefit AND cost
explicitly. Never omit the cost side.

## III. Digital System Architecture
Describe each tool precisely, without overstating its capabilities:
- Website platform: state control benefits accurately (e.g. technical SEO
  control), never claim a CMS itself guarantees rankings
- Booking + payment system: state processing fees as a realistic range
- Email platform: explain automation triggers and list management
- Analytics: explain what decisions the data will actually drive

## IV. Customer Acquisition & Traffic Migration Strategy (3 phases)
Phase 1 (Months 0–3): List capture and lawful migration from the existing
platform — including any existing-customer incentive as an explicit
execution step, not buried under risk mitigation
Phase 2 (Months 3–6): Owned content + organic growth channels, run in
parallel, not sequentially
Phase 3 (Months 6–12): Data-driven retargeting once sufficient audience
data has accumulated

## V. Financial Projections & Risk Management
- Present all figures as ranges, never single precise numbers
- Itemize fixed costs — do not round down or omit minor recurring costs
- Include a risk matrix (minimum 4 items), each with a concrete, actionable
  mitigation — explicitly consider platform policy/ToS risk

# Output style requirements
- No filler openers ("As a...", "First, we will...") — go straight into
  the analysis
- All financial figures as ranges, never single precise numbers
- Each module ends with one "Execution Reminder"
- Markdown headers + bolded keywords + bullet points, no unbroken text blocks

## Key Principles (why this prompt works)

- **Role** — a specific professional identity, not a generic assistant
- **Constraint** — explicit rules set before generation, not corrected after
- **Structure** — a fixed module set for completeness and comparability
- **Critique** — always run a second, adversarial pass before treating
  any draft as final (see full case study for this step in action)
